Forecast accuracy & sources How the local forecast is made

MeteoSwiss ICON-CH2 forecasts, supplied by Open-Meteo, are calibrated to this station for temperature, humidity, wind and gusts. Rain retains the original forecast. Weather codes, rain probability, cloud cover and forecast pressure also come from the original model.

Held-out forecast evaluation
MeasurementLocal mean errorOriginal mean errorChange

Mean absolute error is the average size of a forecast error. These results cover 24, 48, 72 and 96-hour forecast offsets in the test period, not every season. Missing readings are excluded; rain is evaluated in non-freezing conditions. The small wind model improves on the original forecast, but a simple linear correction scored better in this test. Methodology and limitations ↗

Station readings are refreshed approximately every 30 minutes and forecasts every two hours when the scheduled update runs. A cached or delayed update is marked by its timestamp.
